Adobe-Japan1 fonts

  • In Adobe-Japan1 fonts, vert shares the default full-width glyph for ordinary kana. Small kana and the Katakana-Hiragana Prolonged Sound Mark ー are common exceptions.
  • ruby selects glyphs designed for annotations. In Adobe-Japan1 fonts, ruby.vert shares the horizontal ruby glyph for ordinary kana. Small kana and the Katakana-Hiragana Prolonged Sound Mark ー can have separate vertical ruby glyphs.
  • Ruby annotations can use scaled standard kana in Adobe-Japan1-3 fonts. Dedicated ruby glyphs and the direction-tuned hkna and vkna sets were introduced in Adobe-Japan1-4.
  • Many Adobe-Japan1 fonts reuse the standard kana outlines for hkna and vkna or omit these features. Notable exceptions include Hiragino Mincho Pro and ProN, Kozuka Gothic Pr6N, and Kozuka Mincho Pr6N.
  • Adobe-Japan1-5 defines two additional sets of kana glyphs: proportional kana for horizontal (pkna) and vertical (pkna.vert) text, each with its own CIDs.

Source Han series

The pan-CJK Source Han Sans and Source Han Serif have their own Adobe-Identity-0 glyph ordering, independent of the Adobe-Japan1 collection. Both have default horizontal kana and separate, slightly smaller forms for vertical writing. The vert feature selects the vertical forms for both ordinary and small kana. Neither family includes hkna or vkna.

Neither family has dedicated ruby kana glyphs. Their ruby feature substitutes only Bopomofo tone marks. Source Han Sans feature source and Source Han Serif feature source.

Source Han Serif — why it switches glyphs

縦書きへの対応については、「フォントデザイン自体が、横より縦組の方が大きく見える」ことから、「縦書き用のグリフを別に持ち、自動的に切り替わるようにしている」とのこと。

Nishizuka says the design looks larger in vertical setting, so the font carries separate vertical glyphs and switches to them automatically. Jump to the launch report passage.
